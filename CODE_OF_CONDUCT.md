# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the person on the other end is trying to help.

## Specifics

- **Critique the work, not the person.** "This wording lets the agent skip verification" is useful. "This is amateur hour" is not.
- **Field reports are gifts.** Someone taking the time to tell you their run went badly is doing the project a favour. Thank them and ask for details.
- **No harassment**, in any form, in issues, pull requests, commit messages, or anywhere else this project is discussed. This includes personal attacks, unwelcome attention, deliberate misgendering, and publishing anyone's private information.
- **Both languages are first class.** Issues and pull requests in Chinese or English are equally welcome. Do not tell someone to switch languages.
- **Do not paste other people's data.** Bug reports produced by this tool come from real machines. Redact before you post, and do not repost someone else's log excerpt outside the thread it appeared in.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this document, and may ban repeat or severe offenders.

Report problems to **gufan0000@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately. If your report concerns a maintainer, say so and it will be handled by someone else.
